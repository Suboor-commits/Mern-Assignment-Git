What I Learned

  • border-radius: Creates rounded or asymmetric corners.
  • background: Supports solid colours and gradients.
  • box-shadow: Adds depth to a card.
  • Flexbox: Arranges cards and adapts to screen size.
  • :hover: Adds interactive visual feedback.
  • Media queries: Improve the layout on mobile devices.
